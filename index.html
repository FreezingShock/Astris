How a star is ranked

  • 1. All three. Links out, links in, and a similar name. Biggest, brightest, closest in.
  • 2. Two-way link. Each article links to the other.
  • 3. Link and name. One link plus a similar name.
  • 4. One-way link. A single link, out or in.
  • 5. Name only. Similar name, no link. Smallest, at the edge.

Modes

  • Explore live
    Wander. The star you click becomes the centre and the sky regrows around it.
  • Race live
    Two articles, a click counter, a timer and a par.
  • Connect two live
    Type two topics and watch the shortest chain between them light up.

    One pair a day, the same for everyone. Compare paths.

    The map explores itself. A screensaver for the curious.

  • Keyboard travel: / to search, arrows to move between stars live
